Crosspost Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Crosspost is a personal desktop tool. Its only user is its owner, who uses it to upload their own videos to their own social media accounts.

What data Crosspost uses

  • The video files, titles and captions you choose to post.
  • Sign-in tokens from YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. These let Crosspost upload to your accounts.
  • For YouTube: your channel name, shown so you can see which channel is connected.

Where it is stored

All data is stored only on your own computer, in Crosspost's local data folder. Crosspost has no servers and does not collect, sell or share any data with the developer or with third parties.

Where it is sent

Videos and captions are sent only to the platforms you choose to post to, through their official APIs: YouTube, Instagram (Meta) and TikTok. Each platform handles your data under its own privacy policy.

YouTube API Services

Crosspost uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using Crosspost with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove Crosspost's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by clicking Disconnect in Crosspost, which deletes the stored token from your computer.

Deleting your data

Delete Crosspost's data folder on your computer to remove everything it has stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/KevinSund92/crosspost/issues.
