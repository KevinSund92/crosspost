Crosspost Terms of Service

Last updated: November 10, 2026

What Crosspost is

Crosspost is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads videos the user chooses to the user's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, using each platform's official API.

Your content and accounts

  • You are responsible for the videos and captions you post, and for having the rights to them.
  • You must follow the rules of each platform you post to, including the YouTube Terms of Service, Instagram Terms of Use and TikTok Terms of Service.
  • Crosspost only posts when you tell it to. You can disconnect any account at any time.

No warranty

Crosspost is provided as is, without any warranty. Platforms can change their APIs or rules at any time, which may stop uploads from working.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: everything stays on your own computer except the videos you choose to post, which go only to the platforms you pick.
